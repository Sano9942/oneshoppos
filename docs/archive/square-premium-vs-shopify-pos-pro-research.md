# Square POS Premium vs Shopify POS Pro Research

Last updated: 2026-04-08

## Purpose

This document is a current, source-backed comparison of:

- `Square Premium` on the unified `Square Point of Sale`
- `Shopify POS Pro`

The goal is to understand their real capabilities as POS platforms, not just high-level marketing claims.

## Important framing

These two products are not scoped the same way.

- `Shopify POS Pro` is primarily a `retail POS add-on` for Shopify stores. It requires a base Shopify e-commerce plan ($29–$2,000+/mo) on top of the $89/mo POS Pro fee.
- `Square Premium` is a `broader commerce software bundle` built on Square's unified POS and multiple business-specific modes. It is standalone at $149/mo per location with no separate base plan required.

That difference matters because Square Premium covers much more than retail POS alone, and the true cost comparison is not $149 vs $89.

### Shopify POS Pro omnichannel capabilities

This is Shopify POS Pro's biggest strength area. All omnichannel features are Pro only:

- **Buy online, pick up in store (BOPIS)**: Customer buys on website, staff sees and processes pickup orders from POS
- **Curbside pickup**: Extension of BOPIS — customer notifies on arrival, staff brings order
- **Ship from store** (native): Staff fulfill online orders from retail location inventory; print packing slips and shipping labels; assign tracking numbers; ship from POS. Supports USPS, UPS, DHL, FedEx via Shopify Shipping.
- **Local delivery**: Stores act as micro-fulfillment centers for local area delivery
- **Buy in store, ship to customer**: Endless aisle — sell product even if not in stock at that location
- **In-store returns of online orders**: Accept returns at any physical store regardless of original fulfillment channel
- **Send cart**: Email cart link for customer to complete purchase online
- **Unified cart/order history**: One view of every customer's transactions across all channels
- **Omnichannel promotions**: Discounts apply across online + POS
- **Centralized order management**: View, fulfill, manage all online orders from POS interface
- **Packing slips**: Print from POS

Key architectural advantage: Shopify's online and offline share the exact same product catalog, customer database, inventory, and order management system natively — no sync middleware, no third-party connectors, no data mismatch. Most competitors require middleware to achieve this.

## Main conclusions

### Where Square Premium is stronger

Square Premium is stronger if you want one system to cover any combination of:

- retail
- restaurant or bar
- salon / bookings / appointments
- services businesses that need estimates, invoices, or contracts
- broader team operations with scheduling, time tracking, and labor management
- embedded financial services (banking, loans, BNPL)

It is closer to a business operations platform with POS at the center. The financial services moat (Banking, Afterpay, Loans, Instant Deposits) is something no other POS company can replicate — Block owns the entire financial stack.

### Where Shopify POS Pro is stronger

Shopify POS Pro is stronger if your main need is:

- retail POS
- strong ecommerce-to-store unification (architecturally superior — same database by design)
- omnichannel fulfillment (BOPIS, ship-from-store native, endless aisle)
- location-specific pricing and product publishing
- operating retail locations on top of a Shopify online store
- access to the largest third-party app ecosystem for extending capabilities

It is more specialized and cleaner for retail, but it is not trying to be a restaurant POS or service-business operating platform.

### Where neither excels natively

- **Loyalty**: Both require add-ons or third-party apps (Square's is first-party at ~$45/mo; Shopify's is all third-party)
- **Advanced demand forecasting**: Both are weak/transitioning (Stocky dying; Square relies on third-party)
- **Payroll**: Square offers it as an add-on ($35/mo + $6/person); Shopify has nothing
- **Time tracking / scheduling**: Square has it native; Shopify requires third-party apps entirely

## Evaluation guidance

If the business you are evaluating is mostly a retailer:

- Shopify POS Pro is usually the better fit when Shopify ecommerce is already the source of truth.
- Square Premium can still work, but its extra breadth may go unused.

If the business needs multiple verticals or non-retail workflows:

- Square Premium is clearly broader.
- Shopify POS Pro would usually require additional apps, process workarounds, or separate systems for appointments, restaurant operations, or service invoicing/contracts.

If cost predictability matters:

- Square's $149/mo is fully standalone. But loyalty ($45+), marketing ($15+), and payroll ($35+$6/person) are extra.
- Shopify's $89/mo is deceptively cheap — it requires a $29–$2,000+ base plan. But Plus merchants get POS Pro free for 20 locations.

If financial services matter:

- Square is the only POS platform with embedded banking, loans, instant deposits, and owned BNPL. No competitor comes close here.
