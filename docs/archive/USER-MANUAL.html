1. Getting Started

Logging In

  1. Open the app in your browser
  2. Enter your Employee ID or Email and your PIN code
  3. Click Login

Selecting Your Branch

After login, select which branch (outlet) you're working at today. If your store has only one branch, this step is automatic.

Your First Day Checklist

  • Log in and select your branch
  • Open a shift in the POS (required before making sales)
  • Clock in via the Kiosk (required before checkout)
  • Start selling — use the POS to ring up sales
  • At end of day: Close the shift with cash denomination count
  • Review your day on the Dashboard and Reports

2. Dashboard

Sidebar → Overview → Dashboard

The Dashboard is your command center. It shows everything happening in your store at a glance.

What You'll See

  • KPI Cards — Today's sales total, number of transactions, average order value, and comparison to yesterday
  • Operational Readiness — Shows if your shift is open, how many employees are clocked in, and any issues
  • Quick Actions — Shortcuts to common tasks (Open POS, View Reports, etc.)
  • Needs Attention — Alerts for low stock, outlets without shifts, and other issues
  • Sales Trends — Chart showing sales over time
  • Top Categories — Which product categories are selling the most
  • Low Stock Alerts — Products that need reordering
  • Staff Today — Who's clocked in, on break, or clocked out
  • Recent Sales — Latest transactions with quick reprint access
The Dashboard auto-refreshes every 60 seconds. Click the refresh button for instant updates.

3. Point of Sale (POS)

Sidebar → Commerce → POS (opens in new tab)

The POS is where you ring up sales. It opens in a separate browser tab so you can keep it running all day.

Opening a Shift

You must open a shift before making any sales.
  1. When you enter POS, you'll see the Open Shift screen
  2. Enter your opening cash using denomination buttons (Rs 5000, Rs 1000, Rs 500, Rs 100, Rs 50, Rs 20, Rs 10)
  3. Click Open Shift

Making a Sale

  1. Find products — Type in the search bar or scan a barcode
  2. Add to cart — Click a product to add it. Click again to increase quantity
  3. Adjust quantities — Use the +/− buttons on each cart item
  4. Apply discounts (optional):
    • Click the Discount button
    • Choose Percentage (%) or Fixed Amount
    • Enter the value and click Apply
  5. Checkout:
    • Click Checkout
    • Select payment: Cash, Card, or Split Payment
    • For cash: enter amount tendered to see change due
    • For split: enter the cash portion, remainder goes to card
    • Click Complete Sale
  6. Receipt — A receipt popup appears. Print it or close it.

Barcode Scanning

If you have a USB barcode scanner, simply scan any product barcode. The POS automatically detects the scan and adds the product to your cart.

Parked Sales

Need to set a sale aside?

  1. Add items to cart
  2. Click Park Sale
  3. To retrieve: click Parked Sales, find your sale, click Resume

Recent Sales

Click the Recent Sales panel to see latest transactions. From here you can:

  • Reprint any receipt
  • Email a receipt to a customer
  • Start a Return or Exchange

Closing a Shift

  1. Click Close Shift
  2. Count your cash using denomination buttons
  3. The system shows expected cash vs. your count (any variance)
  4. Click Close Shift
Rules:
• You must have an open shift to make sales
• You must be clocked in (via Kiosk) to complete checkout
• If your shift is closed, open a new one to continue selling

7. Returns & Exchanges

Sidebar → Commerce → Returns (or from POS Recent Sales)

Processing a Return

From POS (easiest):

  1. Open Recent Sales panel in POS
  2. Find the sale → click Return/Exchange
  3. Select which items to return (all or specific)
  4. Choose refund method: Cash or Store Credit
  5. Click Process Return

Processing an Exchange

  1. Start a return (as above)
  2. Select Exchange instead of refund
  3. Add the new items the customer wants
  4. System calculates the price difference
  5. A new invoice is generated for the exchange
Stock is automatically adjusted — returned items go back into inventory.

9. Products

Sidebar → Inventory → Products

Adding a Simple Product

  1. Click Add Product
  2. Fill in: Name (required), SKU, Barcode, Category, Brand, Price (required), Cost, Stock, Tax
  3. Click Save

Adding a Product with Variants

For products with sizes, colors, etc.:

  1. Click Add Product and fill in base details
  2. Toggle Has Variants on
  3. Add variants — each needs a name, SKU, price, and stock
  4. Click Save

Bulk Import via CSV

  1. Click Import CSV
  2. Download the CSV Template
  3. Fill in your data and upload
  4. Review import results

15. Purchase Orders

Sidebar → Stock Control → Purchase Orders

PO Workflow

  1. Draft — Created but not sent
  2. Ordered — Confirmed with supplier
  3. Received — Stock arrived (create GRN)
  4. Invoiced — Supplier invoice attached
The system warns (but allows) when a credit supplier's total would exceed their credit limit.

16. Stock Adjustments

Sidebar → Stock Control → Stock Adjustments

Manually adjust stock for damaged goods, stocktake corrections, theft, or found items. Select outlet, product, quantity, and reason. All adjustments are recorded for audit.

17. Stock Transfers

Sidebar → Stock Control → Transfers

Move stock between branches. Select source outlet, destination outlet, products, and quantities. Stock is immediately updated in both locations.

18. Returned Stock

Sidebar → Stock Control → Returned Stock

Track goods sent back to suppliers — defective items, wrong shipments. Record products, reasons, and status.

19. Suppliers

Sidebar → Procurement → Suppliers

Manage your supplier directory: contact details, payment terms, credit limits, and transaction history.

20. Expenses

Sidebar → Procurement → Expenses

Record business expenses (Rent, Utilities, Salary, etc.). Enter amount, category, description, and optionally attach a receipt image. Expenses appear in Accounting reports.

21. Approvals

Sidebar → Procurement → Approvals

Review and approve pending requests. Filter by: Pending, Approved, or Rejected.

23. Accounting

Sidebar → Insights → Accounting

Profit & Loss

Gross Sales − Discounts − Returns = Net Revenue − COGS = Gross Profit − Expenses = Net Profit

Cash Flow

Inflows (sales) − Outflows (expenses, payments) = Net Cash Flow

24. Kiosk (Clock-In Terminal)

Sidebar → Operations → Kiosk (opens in new tab)

Standalone PIN-based terminal for employee clock-in/out. Designed to run on a tablet at the store entrance.

How to Use

  1. Enter your 4-digit PIN
  2. System shows your name and status
  3. Clock In → Start Break → End Break → Clock Out
Important: Employees must be clocked in to complete sales at the POS. A shift must also be open.

32. Printer Settings

Sidebar → Setup → Printer Settings

Connect via USB or TCP/IP. Set paper width (80mm or 58mm). Test print to verify.

Configure SMTP for email receipts: host, port, username, app password, from name. Send test email to verify.

If no printer is configured, receipts open in a browser popup. Use Ctrl+P to print.

34. Backup & Restore

Sidebar → Admin → Backup & Restore

Download Backup: Downloads a complete copy of your database. Store safely.

Restore from File: Upload a backup to restore your data.

Warning: Restoring a backup overwrites ALL current data. Any changes made after the backup was taken will be lost.
Download a backup at least once a week.

Appendix C: Tips & Troubleshooting

Tips

  • Bookmark the POS tab for quick access each morning
  • Keep POS and Kiosk tabs running all day in separate browser tabs
  • Use Ctrl+P for browser printing if no thermal printer is connected
  • Almost every report can be exported to CSV for Excel
  • Use Split Payment to accept both cash and card in one transaction

Troubleshooting

ProblemSolution
"Cannot checkout — employee not clocked in"Go to Kiosk tab and clock in with your PIN
"No active shift"Open a shift in POS or Cash Management
Can't find a feature in sidebarYour role may lack permission — contact Administrator
Product stock shows 0 after addingEnter stock quantity when creating the product
Receipt not printingCheck Printer Settings, or use Ctrl+P as fallback
Report shows no dataCheck date range and outlet filter settings
