## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you sign up and use the Service, we collect:

- **Identity and contact data**: name, email address, phone number, business name, business address, tax registration number (e.g., NTN in Pakistan), and similar information you provide during signup or in your company profile.
- **Authentication data**: passwords (stored as bcrypt hashes — we never see the plaintext), employee PINs (also hashed), and session tokens.
- **Billing data**: subscription plan, billing history, and payment-record metadata. **Full payment-card numbers are handled by our payment provider and are never stored by us.**
- **Support data**: messages, screenshots, and attachments you send to our support team or through the in-app support widget.
- **Configuration data**: outlet details, employee records, role assignments, integration credentials, tax settings, and similar setup information.

### 2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

When you use the Service, we automatically collect:

- **Technical data**: IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, time zone, and language settings.
- **Usage data**: pages viewed, features used, timestamps of actions, and unique request identifiers used for debugging.
- **Error and performance data**: error reports, stack traces, and performance metrics captured by our error-monitoring tool (Sentry). These reports may incidentally include some account data if it is present in the context of an error.
- **Cookies and similar storage**: we use first-party cookies and browser localStorage strictly for authentication, session management, theme preferences, and similar essential functions. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or behavioural-advertising technologies.

### 2.3 Customer Data You Upload

In the course of using the Service, you upload personal data about your own end customers and employees, including:

- Customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, and addresses
- Customer purchase history and loyalty data
- Employee names, contact details, and attendance/payroll records
- Any other personal data you choose to record in customer notes, support tickets, or custom fields

You determine what data to collect, why, and how long to retain it. We process this data on your behalf in accordance with these Terms and any Data Processing Agreement (DPA) we have signed with you.

### 2.4 Information from Third Parties

If you enable a third-party integration (e.g., Shopify, WooCommerce, Daraz, a courier API), we receive data from that third party as necessary to provide the integration — for example, order data, product data, and tracking events. We process this data as part of the Service.

## 8. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

- Encryption in transit using TLS/HTTPS for all communications with the Service;
- Encryption at rest for sensitive data, including AES-256-GCM encryption of integration credentials (e.g., third-party API keys and tokens);
- bcrypt hashing for all user passwords and PINs;
- Role-based access controls within the Service and within our own infrastructure;
- Session management with short-lived access tokens and rotating refresh tokens;
- Audit logging of administrative actions;
- Regular security monitoring and error tracking;
- Background database backups;
- Periodic review of sub-processors and their security practices.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work continuously to maintain industry-standard protections.

### Breach Notification

If we become aware of a personal-data breach affecting your data, we will notify you without undue delay, and where feasible within 72 hours of becoming aware, in line with GDPR-style standards. The notice will describe the nature of the breach, the data involved, the likely consequences, and the measures we are taking.
