### 2.1 Retail & Grocery — `/industries/retail`
- **Meta**: `Retail & Grocery POS — SanoPOS` / `Barcode checkout, weight-based selling, batch & expiry tracking, loyalty and multi-outlet stock — one POS for shops, marts, butchers, sweet shops and fabric stores.`
- **H1**: **Run the counter. Own the stock.**
- Subhead: From a corner shop to a multi-branch mart — barcode lanes, weighted items, expiry-tracked batches and a loyalty program your customers actually join.
- Blocks:
  1. *Checkout* — **Scan, weigh, sell.** Barcode/SKU/price search, PLU rapid-entry pad for loose produce, scale-embedded barcode decoding, tare weights for anything sold by the kilo — meat, sweets, fabric by the meter.
  2. *Freshness* — **Batches that expire before you notice? Not anymore.** Per-batch expiry with first-expiry-first-out picking, expiring-soon alerts and batch reports for perishables.
  3. *Production* — **From carcass to cuts, from trays to gift boxes.** Recipe/kit engine handles disassembly (a carcass into priced cuts) and assembly (an assorted mithai box) with stock that stays true.
  4. *Retail intelligence* — **Know what to reorder before you run out.** ABC classification, velocity, dead-stock and reorder suggestions; cycle counts without closing the shop.
  5. *Customers* — **Loyalty, gift cards, holds.** Points and tiers, gift cards, layaway/holds, and a weekly owner digest in your inbox.
- FAQ: Do you support selling by weight? (Yes — kg/g/lb with tare, plus meters for fabric.) / Can I run more than one branch? (Yes — transfers, per-outlet stock and pricing; outlets are paid add-ons.)
