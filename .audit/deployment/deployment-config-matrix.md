## Required production evidence to close blockers

1. Railway: active deployment revision, replica/memory settings, env variable names (values redacted), worker boot logs, restart history, and health probe results.
2. Supabase: project health/advisors, backup/PITR retention, last successful backup, and a recent isolated restore-drill record.
3. Cloudflare/R2: bucket existence, lifecycle/versioning/retention policy, encryption/access policy, backup-object checksum/readback, and custom-domain/proxy choice.
4. UptimeRobot: monitor URL, interval, current state, incident history, and whether it uses shallow or dependency-aware readiness.
5. Vercel: current production revision and redacted presence/scope of `VITE_API_BASE`, `VITE_MULTI_TENANT`, `VITE_SENTRY_DSN`, and Turnstile settings.
