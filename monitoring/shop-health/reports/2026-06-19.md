## What didn't

### 🔴 Currency is wrong on every shop (`$` instead of `Rs`)
- All four are PKR businesses, but every money figure renders with `$` (e.g. cafe "Retail **$** 217,800.00", POS "Pay **$** 0.00"; retail/restaurant/wholesale dashboards same — `$` count 6–15 per page, `Rs` count 0).
- **Root cause (visible in console):** `GET https://api.sanopos.com/api/company-profile/currency?tenant=<slug>` returns **401 Unauthorized** on all four tenants. With the currency lookup failing, the app silently falls back to `$`.
- **Likely a regression** from the May `?tenant=` auth-lockdown security batch — that endpoint is called before the auth interceptor is wired and relies on the `?tenant=` query param, which the lockdown now rejects.
- **Why it matters:** every live Pakistani merchant's dashboard and POS shows the wrong currency symbol. High visibility, low effort to fix (relax/whitelist the currency endpoint, or have it resolve tenant from JWT).
