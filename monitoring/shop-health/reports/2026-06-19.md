## What didn't

### 🟢 RESOLVED — Currency showed $ instead of Rs (fixed + deployed today)
- Earlier today all four shops rendered money as `$` instead of `Rs`. Cause: `GET /api/company-profile/currency?tenant=…` returned **401** (a public endpoint left off the `?tenant=` lockdown allowlist in the May security batch), so the app fell back to `$`.
- **Fixed** (whitelisted the read-only `/currency` sub-path), merged to main, deployed, and **verified live**: the endpoint now returns `{"symbol":"Rs","code":"PKR",...}` and the cafe POS showed `Rs` throughout. No longer an issue.
