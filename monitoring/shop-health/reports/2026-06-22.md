Clicking it just opens the close-shift form — the variance reason field is still required (labeled "required"), and the manager PIN is also still required because variance exceeds the Rs 500 threshold.
- **Why it matters:** A real cashier would expect this button to just work. Instead they get a form that looks like the regular close shift flow. The button name sets a false expectation.
- **Severity:** Low — the form works correctly, it's just a UX label mismatch.
